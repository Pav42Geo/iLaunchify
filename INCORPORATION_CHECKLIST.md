## The four dates that actually matter

| Deadline | What | Why it hurts |
|---|---|---|
| **Stock issuance + 30 days** | 83(b) elections filed | Statutory. No extensions, no fix. Missing it means tax on paper gains as shares vest. |
| **GA qualification + 90 days** | Georgia initial annual registration | Required for entities registered before Oct 1. Late fee plus risk of administrative dissolution. |
| **March 1, 2027** | Delaware annual report + franchise tax | Penalties and interest, then loss of good standing. |
| **April 15, 2027** | Federal Form 1120 for tax year 2026 | Filed even with zero revenue. |

## No action needed

- **FinCEN beneficial ownership (BOI) reporting.** A FinCEN interim final rule from March 2025 redefined "reporting company" to cover only entities formed outside the US. Companies formed in the US, including your Delaware corporation, are exempt. These rules have moved around a lot, so have your CPA confirm the position at filing time.
