## 1. The reframe (what's new vs POSITIONING.md)

POSITIONING.md positions us as a **production marketplace / orchestration layer**. Pavel wants to lead with the **white- & private-label** frame because that's the language buyers already search and understand (and it's literally true: a creator puts *their* brand on a manufacturer's proven, FDA-ready product, and we run the production).

**Synthesized positioning line:**
> **iLaunchify is the white- & private-label production marketplace for CPG.**
> Creators put their brand on a proven, FDA-compliant product and we run the manufacturing. Manufacturers list their label-ready catalog and fill their floor with branded demand.

This keeps POSITIONING.md's locked one-line available as the sub-headline:
> *Launch a real CPG brand without becoming a CPG operator.*

**Key differentiator vs dropship white-label (Supliful/Blanka):** they print-on-demand a label on a held-inventory bottle. We orchestrate **real production runs** across manufacturer + printer + co-packer + warehouse — a real brand, your own inventory, not dropship. Say "production," not "dropship."

## 2. Target market + two-sided persona map

Two-sided marketplace. **Buyers** create demand (brand + order product). **Sellers** supply it (make + fulfill). Each gets its own surface + first sentence — mixing them blurs everything (POSITIONING.md §2).

| **B2 · Indie CPG Operator** | 1–5 SKUs, $50K–$2M/yr, coordinates 2–4 vendors by hand | `/how-it-works` → `/pricing` | "Four steps you see. Forty handoffs you don't." | *The ops layer your team is becoming — without hiring it.* |

(Agency tier: unlimited brands, 8% fee, dedicated AM.)* |

All sellers list **white/private-label-ready, FDA-compliant products** (or production capacity) and get routed pre-qualified, creator-branded orders by capability, region, and capacity. Surface = `/business`. First words = *"Sell your white-label catalog to branded demand."* The routing engine (MOQ / region / capability tuple) accommodates the full scale range below.

## 5. Competitive messaging insights (white-label platforms)

- **Supliful** (private-label supplements): leads with *"create and sell your brand,"* *"you own the brand, the customer relationship, and the upside — we stay invisible,"* *"launch in days, not months,"* *"FDA-compliant, vetted ingredients, US-based fulfillment,"* *"no inventory."* Pattern = **ownership + invisibility + speed + compliance + no-MOQ/inventory.**
- **Blanka** (white-label beauty): *"create your own cosmetic line,"* *"no MOQs, flexible tiers."*
- **Takeaways for us:** lead with *your brand / you own it / we're invisible* (we already say "we never appear in the consumer flow"), pair speed ("days") with **compliance** (our FDA rendering is a real, ownable edge), and be explicit that the brand is the buyer's, not ours (POSITIONING.md fear: "brand dilution / white-label kit" — our lockup must read as *theirs*).
- **Where we beat dropship white-label:** real production runs (not POD), multi-partner orchestration, owned inventory, manifest-locked approvals — say "production," show the graph.

Sources: [Supliful](https://supliful.com/), [Supliful vs Blanka vs Jubilee](https://supliful.com/blog/supliful-vs-blanka-vs-jubilee-beauty), [Best white-label suppliers 2026](https://dodropshipping.com/best-white-label-dropshipping-suppliers/).
