Tooling & repeat runs New

The single biggest gap I found. Plates and dies are made once and kept. Charging a repeat customer full setup again is wrong, and not charging it on run one is a loss.

One-time per shape. Reused on repeats.
Flexo. One-time per artwork.
Same artwork, plates exist.
!
Why this matters more than it looks: the trade press says "well over 70% of short-run jobs are in fact long-run jobs broken into batches". If repeats re-pay setup, you are pricing your best, stickiest revenue out of the market.
