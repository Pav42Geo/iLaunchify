● Questions

Beta, explained.

Is it really free? +
Yes. While we're in beta, there are no platform fees and no subscription for founding members. We're focused on validating the platform and learning how you use it. Monetization comes later, and founding members keep locked-in pricing when it does.
What do you want in return? +
Your honest feedback. That means the occasional short survey, an optional call now and then, and using the in-app feedback rail when something works well, or doesn't. Your input directly shapes what we build.
Who can join the beta? +
Two groups: Creators (influencers and indie operators launching a CPG brand) and Partners. On the partner side we only accept manufacturers, printers, co-packers, and warehouses that offer white-label, private-label, or custom-formulation production. We do not accept partners who only make their own branded goods. The beta is US-only, invite-only, and released in waves.
How do I actually get in? +
Fill out the short beta interest survey for your track. If it's a fit, we email you an invite with a link to the real sign-up. You don't create a full account until your seat opens.
Will the platform change during beta? +
Constantly, and that's the point. Expect frequent improvements. We snapshot your work and communicate changes, and founding feedback is the biggest driver of what we ship each week.
