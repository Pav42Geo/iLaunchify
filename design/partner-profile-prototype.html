About Northwind

Northwind Nutrition is a full-stack CPG production partner specializing in functional beverages, powders, and encapsulated supplements. We run two FDA-registered, GMP-certified facilities in the Pacific Northwest with in-house formulation, blending, filling, labeling, and a climate-controlled fulfillment center — so a creator's SKU never leaves our ecosystem between recipe and doorstep. We favor small MOQs and fast reorders, making us a fit for early-stage brands scaling from first launch to national retail.
