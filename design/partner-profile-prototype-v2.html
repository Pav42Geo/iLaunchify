About Northwind

Northwind Nutrition is a full-stack CPG production partner — functional beverages, powders, and encapsulated supplements. Two FDA-registered, GMP-certified facilities (Portland & Reno) with in-house formulation, filling, labeling, and a climate-controlled fulfillment center, so a creator's SKU never leaves our ecosystem between recipe and doorstep. We favor small MOQs and fast reorders for early-stage brands scaling to national retail.
